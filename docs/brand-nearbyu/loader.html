Loading, like the opening

The loader is the opening animation on a loop: the rings ripple out, the pin drops in, the road draws itself to the home, the home pops up and the three helpers swing in and pulse. Then it fades softly and plays again, for as long as the app is loading.

0.00–0.38 sRings ripple out 0.00–0.29 sThe location pin drops in with a small bounce 0.19–0.77 sAs it lands, the road draws itself at an even pace from the pin to the home 0.60–0.84 sThe home rises while the road climbs to it, so it is there as the road arrives 0.67–1.25 sThe three helpers swing into place on the rings 1.10–1.82 sEach helper sends out a soft pulse 1.92–2.40 sEverything fades softly and the next loop begins (2.4 s per loop)
