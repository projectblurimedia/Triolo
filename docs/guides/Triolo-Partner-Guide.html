1Register

Step 1

Choose what you are

Open Triolo Partner and pick: Are you a worker? · Do you have a shop? · Looking for a job?

Fill the form: photo, name, mobile (verify with the SMS code), your work or shop kind, place on the map, photos.

Tap Register. Workers and shops are checked by Triolo first (you see Pending, then Verified).

One account can be a worker, own up to 5 shops and have several job posts.

2Jobs (workers)

Step 1

Live Requests and Find Jobs Now

New requests appear as cards on Home — swipe through them, Accept or Decline.

Find Jobs Now pulls in every open request near you.

Requests booked with you personally say "Booked you".

You get some free accepts each month. When they are used up, the button becomes "Subscribe to accept" — take the Service plan to keep accepting.

Step 2

Go, arrive, start with the PIN

Tap Start Trip — the customer sees you coming on the map.

At the place tap Arrived, ask the customer for their 4-digit PIN and enter it.

Do the work, tap Mark Work Done. The customer pays you directly.

Step 2

Pack and make the bill

Tick each item packed and type its price; untick what you don't have.

Add to the bill one way: Add items (type), Record items (say them), Capture items (camera or gallery). Only the way you pick stays.

With a photo or recording you can type one Bill total instead of every price.

Tap Send Bill.
