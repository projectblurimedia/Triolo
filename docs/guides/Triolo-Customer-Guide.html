1Sign up and sign in

Step 1

Choose your language and create your account

Open Triolo, pick English or తెలుగు.

Tap Create Account, type your name and mobile number, tap Verify.

Type the 6-digit code from the SMS. Add a photo, email and your place if you like, then Register.

Already have an account? Tap Log In and use the same number.

2Quick Book — a worker in minutes

Your request goes to every matching worker near you. The first one to accept comes to you.

Step 1

Choose the work and book

Home → Quick Book. Pick one or more kinds of work (electrician, plumber…).

Tap Book Nearby. Turn location on if asked.

Without Triolo Plus you get some free bookings each month, then a small fee per booking. The app tells you before you pay.

4Post a Problem

Step 1

Describe it and say your price

Home → Post a Problem. Add photos, pick who you need, write a short note and your price (quotation).

Choose the location, then Post. Workers nearby see it and one accepts — then it works like a booking with tracking and PIN.

5Prepare a shopping list

Make a list one of four ways. Once you choose a way, only that way stays on screen.

Step 1

Pick how to make the list

Prepare List → New List.

Speak & prepare: say "rice 5 kg, oil 1 litre" and items appear.

Add items: type them one by one.

Photo of list: take a photo of a list written on paper.

Record voice: just say what you want — the shop listens to it.

Step 2

Record a voice list

Tap Record voice, speak up to 3 minutes, tap the red stop button.

Play it back, delete or Record again. Then Save List.

A list with no name gets one like "Voice list · 2 Nov, 4:35 PM". Deleting everything from a saved list deletes the list.

Step 2

See the delivery charge, then the bill

As soon as a shop accepts a delivery order you see its delivery charge.

The shop packs and sends the bill: items with prices (or one total), what is not available, any photos or voice message from the shop.

Your own list (your recording or photos) is shown first, then the bill.

Settings, privacy and deleting your account
