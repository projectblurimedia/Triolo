3Review, results and refunds

Step 1

Review

Triolo checks every ad. Approved: it starts on its date. Rejected: you see the reason and your money is refunded automatically to the same UPI / card (5–7 working days).

You can withdraw an ad before it is approved — refunded the same way.

4Rules

Step 1

What is not allowed

Nothing false, illegal, adult, political, alcohol, tobacco, gambling or betting.

No other people's photos or logos without permission.

Your ad must match what you sell. Triolo may reject or remove ads that break these rules.
